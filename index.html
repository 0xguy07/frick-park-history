Frick Park History Map

About this map
A community history map of Frick Park, Pittsburgh's largest city park, from the Swisshelm mill and the Frick estate to the Fern Hollow Bridge. Photos (●; hollow = in copyright, opens at the archive), events (◆), lost features (✕), today's trails (━) and streams (━). Photo spots and many event spots are estimates; click any item for its confidence and source. Events marked "park-wide" have no single spot and appear only in the list. Sources and credits: see the project page. Next door: the Swisshelm Park History Map.
