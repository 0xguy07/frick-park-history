Pictorial Map of Frick Park, 1939

Drawn by Ezra C. Stiles (1938) and published by the Union Trust Co., trustee of the Frick bequest, in March 1939. Shows the trails, shelters, nature museum, "future" tennis courts and parking, and the proposed Lincoln Highway (today's Parkway). It is an illustrated map, not drawn to a single scale, so it appears here as-is rather than laid over today's streets.
